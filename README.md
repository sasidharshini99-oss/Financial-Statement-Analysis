Financial Statement Analysis: Coca-Cola (KO) vs PepsiCo (PEP), FY2021-FY2025

HOW TO USE
1. Open the KO and PEP tabs. Fill every yellow cell (blue text) with figures from each company's 10-K, in $ millions.
2. Source: SEC EDGAR, 10-K filings (Consolidated Statements of Income, Balance Sheets, Cash Flows). Record the page in column G.
3. Everything else (Ratios, DuPont, CommonSize_Trend, Dashboard) calculates automatically. Do not overwrite black or green cells.
4. Use GAAP figures as reported. Where a year was restated, use the comparative figure in the most recent 10-K.

COLOR LEGEND
Yellow fill + blue text = input you type.   Black = formula.   Green = link to another sheet.

EXAMPLE OF EXPECTED INPUT FORMAT (illustrative placeholder values, NOT actual company data)
Line item | FY2021 | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025
Net revenue | 10,000 | 10,500 | 11,000 | 11,500 | 12,000   (whole $ millions, no $ sign; capex, dividends and buybacks as positive numbers)

ASSUMPTIONS AND SIMPLIFICATIONS
- Ratios use year-end balances rather than averages, so FY2021 can be computed without FY2020 data.
- Debt = short-term debt + long-term debt, excluding operating leases. Equity includes non-controlling interests; net income is attributable to the company.
- Days ratios use 365 days. Free cash flow = operating cash flow - capital expenditures.
- Data check: all PEP inputs and all KO inputs were verified against the companies' 10-K filings, except KO FY2021 cash flow items (from a 10-K data summary). PEP interest is NET interest expense (not comparable to KO gross interest). Fiscal year-ends differ slightly (PEP: last Saturday of Dec; KO: Dec 31).
- Divisions by zero or blank inputs return a blank cell rather than an error.
